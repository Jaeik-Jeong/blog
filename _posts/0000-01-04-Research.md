My research interests are AI in energy and energy-efficient AI.

# __Beyond Forecasting Accuracy: Deep Reinforcement Learning for Renewable Energy Management__<br/>

My research has focused on applying deep learning and reinforcement learning to renewable energy forecasting and energy management. The central idea behind my research is that renewable energy forecasting should consider not only the magnitude of prediction errors but also their compensability. My first study investigated how forecasting errors from multiple photovoltaic sites could offset each other through aggregation, using a convolutional neural network (CNN) to capture spatial and temporal correlations.<br/>
Building on this idea, my second study introduced a new forecasting objective that considers the ability of energy storage systems (ESSs) to compensate for prediction errors. Using proximal policy optimization (PPO) with a long short-term memory (LSTM) network, I developed a deep reinforcement learning framework that minimizes the remaining errors after ESS compensation. My third study further extended this approach by incorporating electricity market bidding strategies, jointly optimizing error compensation and economic profits. I also envisioned integrating the CNN from my first study with the LSTM-based reinforcement learning framework from my subsequent studies, although this direction remained unexplored after completing my Ph.D.<br/>
Beyond these core studies, I investigated the advantages of discrete reinforcement learning over continuous reinforcement learning for ESS energy arbitrage, broadening my perspective on reinforcement learning algorithms. I also contributed to a collaborative study addressing integrated energy management under uncertainty, involving renewable energy, ESSs, and electricity markets. Overall, my research has evolved from improving forecasting accuracy toward integrating forecasting, physical flexibility, and economic decision-making in renewable energy systems.<br/>
